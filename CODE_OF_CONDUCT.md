# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Covalent contributors must keep discussion respectful, specific, and focused on the work. Welcome different backgrounds and levels of experience. Give actionable feedback, acknowledge mistakes, and protect users whose backup or identity data may be involved.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publication of another person's private data are not acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `thekozugroup@gmail.com`. Include links or other evidence needed to understand the incident, but do not send unrelated private data. The maintainer may remove content, restrict participation, or ban an account in proportion to the behavior. Reports and reporter identities will be shared only as needed to investigate and respond.

This policy applies to the repository, issue tracker, review discussions, release channels, and project events. Enforcement decisions may be appealed once by replying to the original private report.
