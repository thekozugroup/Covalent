# Third-party notices

Covalent release artifacts retain upstream license metadata and ship the exact dependency inventories generated by the release workflows. Rust dependencies are recorded in the source SPDX SBOM and `Cargo.lock`; Android dependencies and their bundled license resources are recorded by the Android release tasks; Apple dependencies are recorded by `scripts/apple-dependency-inventory.sh` and `apps/apple/Package.resolved`.
