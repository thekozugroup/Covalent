the copy, copyto, lsjson, serve sftp, sync, and version commands. It uses the
unmodified upstream transfer implementation. The bundled target manifest and
Open Source Notices contain byte-exact license and notice evidence for the
compiled macOS arm64 package graph. That inventory records evidence for review;
it is not legal approval.
